Notes on the current feed

Close. Here's what makes it luxe.

Keep

  • Black and gold. The base is right, and people already link it to 305 SKY.
  • Real photography. The Challenger at sunset, the cabin, the techs on the King Air and the panel are strong. They are the hero of every new layout above.
  • The serif headlines. "The Engine Records Matter" and "Transform The Cabin" already feel high end.
  • The client testimonial from a 15-year private jet owner. It is the most persuasive post on the grid.

Change

  • One type voice. Heavy, condensed all-caps ("GROUNDED?", "WE'RE HIRING!") shouts, which reads as promotional rather than elite. Use the light serif with one italic gold word.
  • Logo smaller, URL off the tile. A large logo and a www.305sky.com box on every post repeat 12 times on a screen. Links can't be tapped on Instagram, so the website belongs in the bio.
  • Fewer words. The small paragraphs can't be read at grid size. Keep 8 words or fewer on the image and put the detail in the caption.
  • Give the grid rhythm. Every tile is equally dark and busy, so nothing stands out. Alternating with ivory (Option A) gives the eye a place to rest.
  • Real over generic. Drop the globe icon, phone mockups and stock-style jets. Show your hangars and people.
  • Tail numbers. N604XT is readable on the "More Than a Fresh Look" post. Blur it unless the owner approved.
