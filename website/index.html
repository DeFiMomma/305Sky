Questions

Owner FAQ.

Aircraft management and maintenance with 305 SKY, answered.

What does 305 SKY do?

305 SKY provides private aircraft management and maintenance for business jets and turboprops, including scheduled inspections, unscheduled repairs, AOG support, pre-buy inspections, records review and Garmin avionics installations.

Where are 305 SKY's locations?

305 SKY has four Florida locations: Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International (FLL), Fort Lauderdale Executive (FXE), Boca Raton (BCT) and Vero Beach (VRB).

Can you maintain large-cabin jets like the Gulfstream G450 or Global Express?

Yes. Our 12,000+ square foot Vero Beach hangar takes mid-size and heavy business jets, including the Dassault Falcon 900, Gulfstream G450 and Bombardier Global Express.

Do you provide AOG support?

Yes. Aircraft on ground (AOG) situations take priority. Call us with the tail number, location and the problem, and we will tell you what we can do and how quickly.

What does aircraft management include?

Flight crew coordination, flight scheduling and logistics, maintenance planning and oversight, insurance and warranty support, owner reporting and budgeting, and fuel, parking and hangar coordination, with one point of contact for your aircraft.

How long has 305 SKY been in business?

305 SKY was founded in 2019, and our team brings more than 55 years of combined generational experience in aviation maintenance and aircraft management.
