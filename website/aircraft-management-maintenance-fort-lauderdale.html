Aircraft maintenance

Aircraft Maintenance in Fort Lauderdale

From planned inspections to the squawk that shows up the night before a trip, our Fort Lauderdale technicians keep your aircraft flying.

Scheduled inspections

Annual, 100-hour and manufacturer-program inspections, including Challenger 400-hour checks and MU-2 special inspections, quoted at a flat rate.

AOG & unscheduled repairs

When your aircraft is down away from home or before a departure, call us. We troubleshoot quickly, source parts and get you back in the air.

Squawks & discrepancies

We work through your squawk list and tracking-system discrepancies, then give you a clear write-up of what was found and fixed.

Engines & powerplant

Engine compressor washes, starter-generator removal and replacement, and routine powerplant servicing on turbine engines.

Gear, tires & brakes

Tire and wheel changes, brake service and landing gear inspection, with the parts sourced and on hand before your aircraft arrives.

Avionics troubleshooting

We troubleshoot intermittent displays and system faults, such as MFD problems, and fix them or coordinate a specialist shop.

Airframe & de-ice

Airframe repairs, panel replacement and de-ice boot repair, plus cabin and galley items so the aircraft is ready for passengers.

Parts sourcing

We source, quote and track OEM and FAA-PMA parts and exchanges so your downtime stays as short as possible.

Aircraft management

Aircraft Management in Fort Lauderdale

Owning an aircraft shouldn't feel like a second job. Our aircraft management service gives you one point of contact who keeps your aircraft legal, ready and on budget.

Maintenance tracking

We monitor your inspection intervals, recurring items and open discrepancies in your maintenance tracking program (such as CAMP), so nothing comes due by surprise.

Inspection planning & budgeting

We forecast upcoming inspections and big-ticket items, schedule downtime around your flying and quote the work ahead of time.

Records management

Logbook entries, work orders and invoices are kept organized and complete, which protects your aircraft's value when it's time to sell.

Vendor & parts coordination

We coordinate outside shops, parts suppliers and FBOs for you and review their invoices before they reach you.

Ferry coordination

We arrange ferry pilots to bring your aircraft to Fort Lauderdale for maintenance and return it when the work is done.

One accountable contact

One call or email gets you a status update on your aircraft, with no chasing multiple vendors and no guessing.

FAQ

Aircraft maintenance & management FAQ

Where is 305 SKY located?

305 SKY is at 240 SW 34th St, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33315, next to Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport (FLL). We also travel to aircraft at other South Florida airports.

What aircraft do you maintain?

We maintain business jets and turboprops, including the Bombardier Challenger 604, Bombardier Global Express (BD-700), Beechcraft King Air B300 and Mitsubishi MU-2. Call us about other types.

Do you handle AOG situations?

Yes. Aircraft on ground (AOG) jobs go to the top of our board. Call us with the tail number, location and the problem, and we will tell you what we can do and how fast.

How is inspection work priced?

Scheduled inspections are quoted at a flat rate based on the manufacturer's inspection program. Discrepancies found during the inspection are quoted separately and are only worked after you approve them.

What does aircraft management include?

Our aircraft management covers maintenance tracking, inspection forecasting and budgeting, records management, parts and vendor coordination, and ferry coordination, with one point of contact for your aircraft.

Can you work on my aircraft at another airport?

Yes. We travel to aircraft at airports across South Florida, including Fort Lauderdale Executive (FXE), Pompano Beach (PMP), Boca Raton (BCT), Miami-Opa locka Executive (OPF) and Palm Beach International (PBI). Travel and any FBO fees are billed at cost.
