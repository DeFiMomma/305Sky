Questions

Owner FAQ.

Aircraft management and maintenance in Fort Lauderdale, answered.

Where is 305 SKY located?

305 SKY is at 240 SW 34th St, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33315, at Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport (FLL). We serve aircraft owners and operators throughout South Florida.

What does aircraft management with 305 SKY include?

Flight crew coordination, flight scheduling and logistics, maintenance planning and oversight, insurance and warranty support, owner reporting and budgeting, and fuel, parking and hangar coordination, all handled by one team with one point of contact.

Do managed aircraft receive preferred maintenance pricing?

Yes. Aircraft managed by 305 SKY receive preferred pricing on maintenance performed by our Fort Lauderdale team, along with fuel discounts through participating providers, preferred parking where available, and access to our South Florida hangar network, subject to availability.

What aircraft do you maintain?

We maintain business jets and turboprops, including the Bombardier Challenger 604, Bombardier Global Express (BD-700), Beechcraft King Air B300 and Mitsubishi MU-2. Contact us about other types.

Do you provide AOG support?

Yes. Aircraft on ground (AOG) situations take priority. Call us with the tail number, location and the problem, and we will tell you what we can do and how quickly.

How is inspection work priced?

Scheduled inspections are quoted at a flat rate based on the manufacturer's inspection program. Anything found during the inspection is quoted separately and only worked after you approve it.
