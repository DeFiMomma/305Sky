Questions

Owner FAQ.

AOG maintenance, pre-buys, avionics and aircraft management in Boca Raton, answered.

Where is the 305 SKY Boca Raton office?

Our Boca Raton office is at 3300 Airport Rd, Ste 202, Boca Raton, FL 33431, at Boca Raton Airport (BCT).

Do you provide AOG maintenance in Boca Raton?

Yes. Aircraft on ground (AOG) situations take priority. Call us with the tail number, location and the problem, and we will tell you what we can do and how quickly, whether the aircraft is at Boca Raton or elsewhere in South Florida.

What is included in a pre-buy inspection?

A pre-buy inspection combines a detailed review of the aircraft's records with a physical inspection of the airframe, engines, systems and avionics. You receive a written report of our findings so you, your broker and your lender can make an informed decision before closing.

Can you review aircraft records without a full inspection?

Yes. We offer stand-alone aircraft records review, covering logbooks, airworthiness directive and service bulletin compliance, inspection status and damage history, for buyers, sellers and owners preparing for a sale.

Do you install Garmin avionics?

Yes. We perform Garmin avionics installations and upgrades, from flight displays and navigators to autopilots and ADS-B, and handle the required installation paperwork.

Can 305 SKY manage my aircraft from Boca Raton?

Yes. Our Boca Raton office offers full aircraft management: flight crew coordination, scheduling and logistics, maintenance planning and oversight, insurance and warranty support, owner reporting and budgeting, and fuel, parking and hangar coordination, with one point of contact.

Do you have other locations?

Yes. In addition to Boca Raton, 305 SKY has locations at Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International (FLL), Fort Lauderdale Executive (FXE) and Vero Beach (VRB), where our 12,000+ square foot hangar takes mid-size and heavy jets.

How long has 305 SKY been in business?

305 SKY was founded in 2019, and our team brings more than 55 years of combined generational experience in aviation maintenance and aircraft management.
