Questions

Owner FAQ.

Mid and heavy jet maintenance in Vero Beach, answered.

Can your Vero Beach hangar fit a Gulfstream G450 or Global Express?

Yes. Our 12,000+ square foot Vero Beach hangar takes mid-size and heavy business jets, including the Dassault Falcon 900, Gulfstream G450 and Bombardier Global Express, so your aircraft is worked on inside, out of the Florida sun and weather.

What jet maintenance do you perform in Vero Beach?

Everything from routine and scheduled maintenance to unscheduled repairs and AOG support, plus pre-purchase inspections and records review, avionics and aircraft systems, and cabin interiors.

Do you provide AOG support on the Treasure Coast?

Yes. Aircraft on ground (AOG) situations take priority. Call us with the tail number, location and the problem, and we will tell you what we can do and how quickly, whether the aircraft is in Vero Beach or elsewhere on the Treasure Coast.

Can 305 SKY manage my jet from Vero Beach?

Yes. We offer full-service aircraft management from Vero Beach: flight crew coordination, scheduling and logistics, maintenance planning and oversight, insurance and warranty support, owner reporting and budgeting, and hangar coordination, with one point of contact.

Do you have other locations?

Yes. 305 SKY also operates at Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport (FLL), so owners who fly between South Florida and the Treasure Coast can rely on one team at both ends.
