# 305 SKY: 90-day Google Maps plan (FLL, BCT, VRB)

**Goal:** appear in the top 3 of Google's map results ("map pack") for core searches near
each location. Example searches:

- **FLL:** "aircraft maintenance Fort Lauderdale", "aircraft management Fort Lauderdale"
- **BCT:** "AOG aircraft maintenance Boca Raton", "pre-buy inspection Boca Raton", "Garmin avionics Boca Raton"
- **VRB:** "jet maintenance Vero Beach", "aircraft maintenance Vero Beach"

## How Google ranks the map pack

Google weighs three things:

1. **Relevance:** how well a profile and website match the search (categories, services, page content).
2. **Distance:** how close the business is to the person searching. You can't change this, so
   nobody ranks top 3 for every searcher everywhere. Expect top-3 results near each airport
   first, then spreading outward.
3. **Prominence:** reviews, links, mentions and listings across the web.

The website pages handle relevance. Most of the work below is on profiles, reviews and
prominence.

**Realistic expectations:** Boca Raton and Vero Beach searches have few direct competitors,
so top-3 within 90 days is realistic. Fort Lauderdale has many maintenance shops at FLL and
FXE, so there it's more likely to take 90 days or longer. No one can honestly guarantee a
ranking.

## Ethical rules (breaking these risks suspension)

- **Name:** exactly "305 SKY" on every profile. Never add keywords like "305 SKY Aircraft
  Maintenance Fort Lauderdale".
- **Locations:** only real, staffed locations with signage get a profile. No virtual offices
  or addresses you don't operate from. FXE gets a profile only if it meets this.
- **Reviews:** no fake reviews, no paying or rewarding customers for reviews, and no asking
  only happy customers ("review gating"). Ask every customer.
- **Photos:** real photos of your facilities, team and work only.
- **One profile per location.** Remove or merge any duplicates.

## Days 1–14: foundation

**Google Business Profile (FLL, BCT, VRB):**
- [ ] Claim and verify each profile. If customers don't come to the BCT suite, set BCT up as
      a service-area business with the address hidden.
- [ ] **Primary category:** *Aircraft maintenance company*. Secondary categories where they
      fit: *Aircraft management company* if offered, *Aviation consultant*, *Avionics shop*
      (BCT only, since it installs Garmin).
- [ ] Use the same name, address, phone and hours as the matching website page. Use a local
      number for each location (954 / 561 / 772) if possible.
- [ ] **Website link:** point each profile at its own location page, not the homepage. Add
      tracking so you can see the visits, e.g.
      `…/aog-maintenance-aircraft-management-boca-raton-fl/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp-bct`
- [ ] **Services:** list every service per location, matching the page (AOG maintenance,
      pre-buy inspections, records review, Garmin avionics installation, aircraft management,
      mid & heavy jet maintenance…).
- [ ] **Description:** 750 characters, written naturally, naming the services and the airport.
- [ ] **Photos:** at least 10 per location: exterior with signage, hangar, team, aircraft
      (with owner permission), lobby. The VRB hangar with the 305 SKY sign is the strongest.
- [ ] Set opening hours and add a note that AOG is available by phone.

**Website:**
- [ ] Launch the homepage and the three location pages, with all placeholders filled.
- [ ] Verify the site in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, and submit the sitemap.
- [ ] Run every page through the Rich Results Test (search.google.com/test/rich-results).
- [ ] For each map embed, use the code from that location's profile (Share → Embed a map).

## Days 15–45: reviews and citations

**Review program:**
- [ ] Create each location's review link (in Business Profile, "Ask for reviews").
- [ ] Ask every customer at job completion: in person, plus a short follow-up email or text
      with the link. Put a "review us" link or QR code on invoices for the right location.
- [ ] **Target:** 10–15 new reviews per location over 90 days, arriving steadily rather than
      in bursts. Ask reviewers to mention the aircraft and the work ("G450 inspection in Vero Beach").
- [ ] Reply to every review within 48 hours, mentioning the service and location naturally.

**Citations (same name, address and phone everywhere):**
- [ ] Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Yelp, BBB, LinkedIn company page, Facebook, Instagram.
- [ ] Aviation directories: AirNav (services at FLL, BCT, VRB), AC-U-KWIK, GlobalAir.com,
      FlightAware airport services, AvBuyer.
- [ ] Data aggregators (e.g. Data Axle, Foursquare), which feed many smaller directories.
- [ ] Fix any old or incorrect listings: wrong phone numbers, old addresses, duplicates.

**Profile activity:**
- [ ] Post on each profile weekly: a recent job (no tail numbers), a tip, a team or hangar
      photo, an AOG save.
- [ ] Add 2–3 new photos per location per week.

## Days 46–90: prominence and content

**Local links and mentions:**
- [ ] Ask each airport to list 305 SKY in its tenant or services directory. Airport websites
      are often government sites, which carry strong local weight.
- [ ] Get listed by FBOs you work with at each airport, plus aircraft brokers, insurance
      agents and flight schools.
- [ ] Join and get listed by the Greater Fort Lauderdale, Boca Raton and Indian River County
      chambers.
- [ ] Owner groups for the types you work on (King Air, MU-2, Falcon, Gulfstream, Global).
- [ ] Local press or aviation media: a story about the Vero Beach hangar or the 2019-founded,
      55+ years of combined generational experience angle.

**Website content (one new page or article every 2 weeks):**
- [ ] Aircraft-type pages, e.g. "Gulfstream G450 maintenance", "Falcon 900 maintenance",
      "Global Express maintenance", "King Air maintenance", each linking to the right location.
- [ ] Short job stories: "AOG recovery at BCT", "400-hour check on a Challenger 604 at FLL".
- [ ] An FXE page, once FXE is confirmed as a staffed location.

## Tracking (check every 2 weeks)

- **Map rank grid:** track your rank on a grid around each airport for 5–10 core searches.
  Tools like Local Falcon or BrightLocal do this; take a baseline in week 1.
- **Business Profile performance:** calls, website clicks and direction requests per location.
- **Search Console:** clicks and impressions for searches containing "fort lauderdale",
  "boca raton" and "vero beach".
- **Reviews:** count and average rating per location against the target.
