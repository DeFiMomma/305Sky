# Platform Playbook

Specs and algorithm behaviour change often. Figures below were gathered October 2026 from
marketing benchmarks (Socialinsider, Buffer analyses, agency guides) — directional, not gospel.
Re-check with a quick web search when a spec matters (e.g. before a paid campaign).

## Cadence (sustainable baseline for a small shop)
| Platform | Posts / week | Format mix | Stories |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 3–4 | 1–2 Reels, 1–2 carousels, occasional single image | 3–5 days/week, raw behind-the-scenes |
| LinkedIn (company page) | 2–3 | text+image, document carousel (PDF), native video | — |
| LinkedIn (owner/leader personal) | 1–2 | personal insight posts; reshares company hero piece | — |
| Facebook | 2–3 | repurposed Reels, photo albums, local/community, events | cross-post IG Stories |

Benchmarks for context: carousels currently earn the highest Instagram engagement rate
(≈0.5%); Reels earn the most reach to non-followers. Leaders in our niche post far less often
than consumer brands — consistency and quality matter more than volume.

## Instagram
- **Sizes:** Feed 4:5 (1080×1350) — profile grid now previews at 3:4, so keep key content central.
- **Grid:** follow the chosen pattern in `visual-identity.md` (default: checkerboard, dark = image, ivory = words).
  Reels/Stories 9:16 (1080×1920), keep text inside the central safe zone (avoid top 250px / bottom 400px).
- **Reels:** hook on screen in the first 1.5 s; 7–30 s for reach, up to 90 s for walkthroughs;
  burned-in captions; original footage only (re-uploads and other apps' watermarks get down-ranked).
  Cover frame designed to match the grid.
- **Carousels:** 6–10 slides. Slide 1 = hook/promise, slide 2 = context, middle = value,
  last = save/share/DM CTA. Consistent template so the series is recognizable.
- **Caption:** first line = hook (≈125 chars visible). Then 2–6 short lines of value, a CTA,
  then hashtags. Use keywords people search ("Challenger 604 maintenance Fort Lauderdale") —
  Instagram search is keyword-driven now.
- **Hashtags:** 3–5 max (Instagram caps hashtags at 5), relevant > popular. Mix: 1 niche-type
  (#Challenger604), 1 audience (#BizAv / #BusinessAviation), 1 local (#FortLauderdale / #FLL),
  1 branded (#305SKY).
- **Location tag:** Fort Lauderdale–Hollywood International Airport (FLL) on every post.
- **Collabs:** use Instagram Collab posts with customers/partners (with permission) to share reach.
- **Engagement:** reply to every comment within the first hour; spend 15 min/day engaging with
  owners, operators, FBOs and aviation photographers in South Florida.

## LinkedIn
- **Audience:** DOMs, chief pilots, management companies, brokers, recruits.
- **Formats that work:** document carousels (upload PDF, 1080×1350 or 1:1, 5–12 pages);
  text post + one strong photo; native vertical/square video ≤ 90 s with captions.
- **Copy:** first ~200 chars show before "…more" — lead with the insight or number.
  Short paragraphs, 1–2 sentences each, white space. 600–1,300 characters is the sweet spot.
  Professional, first-person-plural ("Our team…") on the company page; first-person on personal profiles.
- **Links:** external links in the post body can reduce reach — put the link in the first comment
  or make the post self-contained.
- **Hashtags:** 0–3, professional (#BusinessAviation #AircraftMaintenance #MRO).
- **Amplify:** owner and team reshare with a personal line within the first hour; tag partners/people when genuine.
- **Personal > page:** leaders' personal profiles usually out-reach company pages several times over —
  plan 1 personal post/week from the owner.

## Facebook
- **Audience:** owners, local community, families of the team, older pilot/owner demographic, recruits.
- **Formats:** Reels (cross-post from IG), photo albums ("This week in the hangar"), events, milestones,
  team celebrations, reviews/testimonials.
- **Copy:** warmer and more conversational than LinkedIn; 1–3 short paragraphs; one question to
  invite comments. 0–3 hashtags.
- **Local:** tag local businesses/FBOs/airport pages when relevant; post in relevant groups only
  if rules allow and the content is genuinely helpful (no spam).
- Keep Google Business Profile in sync (same photos, posts) — it drives local search for "aircraft maintenance near me".

## Repurposing chain (one hero asset → 5+ posts)
1. Film a 60–90 s walkthrough (e.g. "What happens in a Challenger 604 inspection").
2. IG Reel: 20–30 s cut with on-screen text.
3. IG carousel: 8 stills + key points.
4. LinkedIn: document carousel with the technical detail + a takeaway for DOMs.
5. Facebook: the Reel + a photo album.
6. Stories: poll/quiz from the same topic ("Which squawk is most common on a 604?").
7. Owner's LinkedIn: personal lesson from the job.

- LinkedIn: Tue–Thu 08:00–10:00.
