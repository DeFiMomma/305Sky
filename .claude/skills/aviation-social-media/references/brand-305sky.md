## Voice
- **Confident, calm, precise.** The tone of a trusted chief inspector, not a salesperson.
- **Miami polish, hangar honesty.** Premium and warm (a nod to 305 / South Florida), never flashy or slangy.
- Use correct terminology (A&P, IA, AOG, RTS, squawk, discrepancy, phase/inspection, logbook entries),
  and explain it in one line on Instagram/Facebook where owners may not know it.
- Write to "you" (the owner, the DOM). Short sentences. Specific numbers.
- Avoid: "world-class", "unmatched", "second to none", "passion for aviation", fear-mongering about safety,
  disparaging other shops.

## Visual identity
- Palette: **black, metallic gold (#C9A24A-ish gradient as in logo), white**; accents from sky blue/teal sparingly (South Florida).
- Photo style: clean ramp and hangar shots, golden hour on the FLL ramp, close-up detail (safety wire,
  torque stripe, gauges, tooling), human hands at work, technicians in clean branded uniforms.
- Logo: white or gold version on dark backgrounds, bottom corner, small; never over the aircraft's livery.
- Typography for overlays: one clean sans (e.g. Montserrat/Inter) + the script "Sky" style only as accent.
- Grid rhythm (Instagram): alternate wide aircraft shots ↔ detail/people ↔ text-led carousel covers.
