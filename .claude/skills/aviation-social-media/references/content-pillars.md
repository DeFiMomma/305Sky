## Recurring series (recognizable formats build habit)
- **Squawk of the Week** (IG carousel / LI doc) — a real discrepancy, what caused it, how it was
  fixed, what owners should watch for. No tail numbers.
- **Torque & Tell** / **Detail Shot Friday** (IG single image or 7-s Reel) — one macro photo of
  quality work with a two-line explanation.
- **Meet the Crew** (IG carousel + FB) — name, role, certifications, years on type, favorite aircraft, why 305.
- **Owner's Minute** (IG Reel / FB) — 30–45 s answer to an owner question ("What's an AOG?",
  "What does 'deferred' mean on my invoice?", "Why do inspections find more than expected?").
- **On the Ramp** (IG) — golden-hour aircraft departing after return to service: "Back in the sky."
- **Quote vs. Invoice** (LI + IG carousel) — how 305 SKY keeps final invoices close to the quote; anonymized example.
- **DOM Notes** (LinkedIn) — planning tips: scheduling inspections around trips, AOG prep kits, records best practices.

## Post templates

**Instagram carousel (Owner's Education)**
1. Cover: big hook (≤8 words) over a strong photo
2. Why it matters (1 line)
3–7. One point per slide, ≤25 words, a supporting detail photo
8. Summary / checklist
9. CTA: "Save this for your next inspection • Questions? DM us"
Caption: hook line → 2–3 lines → CTA → hashtags.

**Reel script (Inside the Hangar, ~20 s)**
0–1.5 s: on-screen hook + strongest motion shot · 1.5–12 s: 4–6 quick cuts of the process ·
12–18 s: result/reveal · 18–20 s: logo end card + CTA text. Burned-in captions throughout.

**LinkedIn text post (DOM Notes)**
Line 1: insight or number. Lines 2–6: context, what we did, what it means for operators.
Close: question to invite DOM perspectives. No link in body.

**Facebook post (Crew / Community)**
Warm opener, 2–3 sentences of story, a question, album of 3–6 photos.
