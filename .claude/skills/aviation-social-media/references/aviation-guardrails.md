# Aviation Guardrails — check before anything is published

## Privacy (the #1 risk for a maintenance shop)
- **Tail numbers / registrations:** blur or crop unless the owner gave written permission.
  Many owners use FAA privacy programs (LADD/PIA) precisely to hide movements.
- **Customer names, companies, principals, crew faces:** never without written permission.
- **Interiors:** distinctive custom interiors can identify an owner — treat like a tail number.
- **Invoices/quotes/work orders on screen:** redact customer, aircraft, serial numbers and prices
  unless approved. Anonymize "real examples" (round numbers, generic type).
- **Schedules:** never reveal when a specific aircraft is in the shop or departing.
- Keep a simple permission log: customer, what's allowed (aircraft/type/name/tail), date, who approved.

## Claims
- Certifications, OEM authorizations, ratings, insurance, "FAA-approved" wording: only when
  confirmed in `brand-305sky.md`. A&P/IA are individual certificates — name the person.
- No absolute safety promises ("100% safe", "never fails"). Talk about process, inspection, documentation.
- Turnaround times, stats and savings: only real numbers the owner supplies; say "typical" when it is.
- Warranty: quote it accurately (90 days or 100 FH, whichever first; exclusions apply) and link to terms if detailed.
- Don't name or disparage competitors. Don't use OEM logos in a way implying endorsement.
- Educational content: general information only; add "Always follow your aircraft's approved
  maintenance program" when giving maintenance guidance.

## Safety in visuals
- PPE where required (eye/ear protection, gloves), no jewelry around moving parts.
- No FOD on the floor or in panels; tools controlled; stands, chocks, covers and tags in place.
- No one standing on unapproved surfaces, no open fuel near ignition sources, no unsafe ladders.
- Drone footage near FLL is restricted airspace — only with proper authorization; don't post
  drone shots of the airfield without confirming they were legally flown.
- Airport security: don't show badge details, security doors, codes or restricted-area procedures.

## Music & assets
- Use platform-licensed audio for business accounts (commercial library) — trending tracks are
  often not licensed for business use.
- Only original photos/video or properly licensed images; credit aviation photographers when reposting with permission.
