# 305 SKY: 90-Day Map Pack Plan (FLL · BCT · VRB, plus OPF)

**Day 1 = Monday, October 12, 2026. Day 90 = Saturday, January 9, 2027.**

Goal: get each 305 SKY Google Business Profile into the top 3 of the Google Maps results
("map pack") for its town's aircraft maintenance, inspection and AOG searches, and rank the
aircraft-type pages in normal Google results.

---

Honest expectations:

- Searches in the map pack depend on where the searcher is standing. "Top 3" will mean top 3
  in part of the area around each airport, then growing outward. Track it with a geogrid (below).
- Searches like "Challenger annual inspection" are made by operators all over the country and
  often show normal results, not a map pack. The aircraft-type pages are what win those.
- Reviews take time to build and there is no shortcut that is safe. Days 1–30 set the base,
  Days 31–60 is when movement usually shows, Days 61–90 is the push into the top 3.

## The rules that keep the profiles safe

1. Business name stays **"305 SKY"**. Never add keywords like "Citation Maintenance FLL" to the
   name. It's the most common reason profiles get suspended.
2. One profile per real, staffed location with 305 SKY signage. No profile for OPF until there
   is a real staffed hangar there.
3. Ask every customer for a review. No paying for reviews, no gifts for reviews, no reviews from
   employees or family, no only asking the happy ones.
4. Only list "Open 24 hours" if a person actually answers the AOG line 24/7.
5. Make one big profile change at a time (name, address, primary category). Each may trigger a
   re-verification.

## Your daily 15 minutes (every working day, starting Day 1)

1. **Ask for reviews.** Every aircraft that left the hangar yesterday gets a review request
   (text + email) from the person the customer dealt with. Template below.
2. **Reply to every new review** within 24 hours, naming the service, aircraft and airport
   naturally: "Thanks, John. Glad the Citation XLS phase inspection at FLL wrapped up ahead of
   schedule."
3. **Add 1–3 real photos** to the profile where the work happened: aircraft in the hangar (no tail
   numbers unless the owner agrees), techs at work, the AOG van, signage. Taken on a phone at the
   location is ideal.
4. **Answer the AOG line and messages fast.** Calls, website clicks and direction requests from the
   profile are signals Google sees.

Weekly (Monday): one Google post per profile (job spotlight, AOG callout, seasonal inspection
reminder). Every second Monday: run the geogrid and update the scoreboard.

### Review request template

> Hi [Name], thanks for trusting 305 SKY with your [Challenger 300 annual inspection] at [FLL].
> If you have a minute, a Google review mentioning the work we did helps other operators find us:
> [short review link for that location]. Thank you! [Your name]

Get each location's short review link from the profile ("Ask for reviews"). Put the right link on
invoices, the email signature, a QR card in each aircraft at delivery, and the AOG van.

## Days 61–90: Push into the top 3 (Dec 11–Jan 9)

- **Weakest location first.** Most of the effort (reviews, photos, posts, links) goes to it.
- **Year-end inspection content.** December–January is when owners plan annual inspections. Weekly
  posts: "Book your Challenger / Citation / King Air annual for Q1", with a quote link.
- **Grid gaps.** Look at where on the geogrid you're weakest (e.g. north of BCT). If there is an FBO,
  flight school or business in that area you work with, ask for a link or mention.
- **Behavior.** Make it easy to call: tap-to-call AOG button on every page, call tracking on each
  profile so you can see which keywords drive calls.
- **Competitor check.** If a competitor's profile breaks Google's rules (keyword stuffing in the
  name, a location with no staffed office), report it through Google's Business Redressal form.
  Only report real violations.
- **Day 90 (Sat Jan 9): Final check-in.** Full scoreboard and geogrid. Plan the next 90 days
  (usually: keep the review pace, add the OPF location if it exists, expand content).
