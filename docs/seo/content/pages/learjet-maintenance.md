## Learjet inspections

We inspect Learjets to the manufacturer's maintenance program, combining hourly, cycle and
calendar tasks to keep downtime short. You get a written quote first, daily updates with photos,
and approval on anything beyond the quote.

## Keeping an out-of-production jet flying

With production over, parts availability matters as much as labor. We source parts through our
supplier network as soon as a finding is confirmed, and we tell you early if a part will drive the
schedule, so you can plan.
