# Aircraft Maintenance, Inspections & Management at Opa-locka (OPF)

[Call (305) 326-3498] [Request a quote]

Miami-Opa Locka Executive Airport is home to some of South Florida's busiest business aviation
operators. 305 SKY serves aircraft at OPF with mobile technicians, a 24/7 AOG line and the
hangars at our [Fort Lauderdale](/fort-lauderdale-fll-aircraft-maintenance),
[Boca Raton](/boca-raton-bct-aircraft-maintenance) and
[Vero Beach](/vero-beach-vrb-aircraft-maintenance) locations.

## How we serve OPF

- **At your aircraft:** our technicians and mobile van come to OPF for squawks, troubleshooting,
  line maintenance and AOG.
- **At our hangars:** for larger inspections, the aircraft makes a short hop to FLL (about 15
  minutes) or BCT, and we can arrange the repositioning.
- **Aircraft management:** we track your maintenance and coordinate the work wherever your
  aircraft is based.

## Aircraft maintenance for OPF operators

Scheduled maintenance, troubleshooting, squawk repair, avionics, interiors and paint on
[Challenger](/challenger-maintenance), [Citation](/citation-maintenance),
[Learjet](/learjet-maintenance), [King Air](/king-air-maintenance),
[Gulfstream](/gulfstream-maintenance), [Falcon](/falcon-maintenance) and
[Global Express](/global-express-maintenance) aircraft.

## Aircraft inspections for OPF operators

Hourly, calendar, phase and annual inspections to the manufacturer's program, plus pre-purchase
inspections for buyers at OPF. You get a written quote first and daily updates.

## Aircraft management at OPF

For owners based at Opa-locka, 305 SKY keeps track of what's coming due, schedules work around your
flying, handles vendors and parts, and keeps your logbooks digitized and organized.
[More about aircraft management](/aircraft-management).
