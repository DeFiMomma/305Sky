## Citation inspections

Most Citations follow Textron's phase inspection program, which spreads inspection tasks into
numbered phases by hours and calendar time so no single visit is enormous. We'll look at your
tracking, tell you which phases and calendar items are coming due, combine what makes sense, and
give you a written quote before starting.

During the inspection you'll get daily updates. Every finding is photographed and priced, and
nothing beyond the quote is done without your approval.
