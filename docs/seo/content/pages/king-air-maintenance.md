## King Air inspections: phase or annual

King Air owners usually choose between:

- **Phase inspections:** Textron's program splits the inspection into Phases 1–4, done at set
  hour intervals, so the aircraft is down for shorter visits more often. Common for aircraft that
  fly a lot.
- **Annual inspection:** everything at once, once a year. Common for aircraft that fly fewer hours.

Not sure which fits how you fly? We'll look at your hours and mission and recommend one. Either
way you get a written quote first and daily updates.
