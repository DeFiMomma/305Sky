## Challenger inspections

Owners often ask for a "Challenger annual inspection." Challengers are maintained under the
manufacturer's inspection program, which schedules tasks by flight hours, cycles and calendar
time. In practice, the "annual" is the set of calendar tasks that come due every 12 months, often
combined with hourly tasks so the aircraft is down once instead of twice.

We'll:

1. Review your tracking (CAMP or similar) and tell you exactly what's coming due
2. Bundle tasks so the aircraft spends as little time on the ground as possible
3. Give you a written quote before we start
4. Send daily updates with photos of anything we find, and price every finding before we fix it
5. Return the aircraft with the records complete and the invoice matching the quote
