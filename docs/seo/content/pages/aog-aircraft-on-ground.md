## What happens when you call

1. **A person answers.** Tell us the aircraft type, the problem and where it's parked.
2. **We give you a real plan.** What we think it is, who's coming, and when.
3. **We come to you** with the van, tools and, when we can, the likely parts.
4. **We source parts fast** through our supplier network if the fix needs them.
5. **You get updates** until the aircraft is signed off and flying.
