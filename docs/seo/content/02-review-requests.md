# Review request kit (daily, from Day 1)

Get each location's review link in the profile: **Home → Ask for reviews → copy link**. Every
customer gets the link **for the location where the work was done**.

| Location | Review link |
|---|---|

| BCT | _paste here_ |
| VRB | _paste here_ |

Rules: ask every customer, not just the happy ones. No discounts or gifts for reviews. No reviews
from employees or family. Never write a review for a customer.

## Text message (send the day the aircraft leaves)

> Hi [Name], it's [Your name] at 305 SKY. Thanks for trusting us with your [Challenger 350
> annual inspection] at [FLL]. If you have a minute, a quick Google review that mentions the work
> we did would mean a lot to our team: [link]

## Follow-up (once, 5 days later, only if no review yet)

> Hi [Name], just following up in case my last message got buried. If you have 60 seconds, a
> Google review would really help us: [link]. Thanks again for flying with 305 SKY.

## Invoice footer / email signature line

> Happy with our work? A Google review helps other operators find us: [location review link]

## QR card (leave on the pilot's seat at delivery)

Front: **Thank you for flying with 305 SKY.**
Back: QR code for that location's review link + "Tell other owners about your [inspection /
repair]. It takes a minute." + (305) 326-3498 · 24/7 AOG

Print a separate stack per location so the QR code always matches.

## Replying to reviews (within 24 hours)

Name the aircraft, the work and the location naturally. Keep it short and human.

**5 stars**
> Thanks, [Name]! Glad the [King Air 350 phase inspection] at [Vero Beach] wrapped up on schedule.
> See you at the next one.

**4 stars**
> Thank you, [Name]. We appreciate you trusting us with your [Learjet 45] at [FLL]. If there's
> anything we could have done better, call me directly at (305) 326-3498. We'd like to earn the
> fifth star.

**Negative**
> [Name], I'm sorry this wasn't the experience you expected. I'd like to understand what happened
> and make it right. Please call me at (305) 326-3498. [Owner name], 305 SKY

Never argue in public, and never share job or pricing details in a reply.
