## Before you publish anything

1. **Strike what isn't true.** Each page lists aircraft models and services. Remove any model or
   inspection you don't actually do.
2. **Fill in the `[brackets]`:** directions, response times, after-hours rates, real reviews,
   photos. Never invent reviews or numbers.
3. **Read the notes at the top of each page** (the `<!-- -->` block): URL, title tag and meta
   description go in your website builder's SEO settings for that page, not in the page text.

## Schema notes

- Replace the three `REPLACE-…` values in each file: a hangar photo URL, the logo URL, and that
  profile's Google Maps share link (Maps → your listing → Share → Copy link).
- The hours say open 24/7 because the AOG line is answered around the clock. If your Google
  profile shows shop hours instead, change these to match the profile exactly.
- Remove any service from the list you've struck from the pages.
- Test at search.google.com/test/rich-results after publishing.

## How the pages link together

Every location page links to every aircraft page and the AOG page; every aircraft page links back
to all three location pages. Keep those links when you build the pages: they tell Google which
services are offered where.
